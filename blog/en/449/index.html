🙏 Don't be afraid, I'm with you

Salute,

Today I’m in a bit of a philosophical mood, let’s step away a little from the engineer and think about it.

I’m sure that you often see the play “Don’t be afraid, I’m with you” on billboards and have your eye on it, but would you like to share what the meaning is?

Few people even understand where this comes from and how important this part is in our lives, look:

“Fear not, for I am with you;

do not be troubled, for I am your God;

I will strengthen you, and help you, and uphold you with the right hand of My righteousness."

— Isaiah 41:10

God says this to the people in exile, that is, to those who have lost everything and do not understand what will happen next, but I am nearby.

It is the phrase “I’m nearby” that is significant and if you hear it, then appreciate it 🙏

In information security, we build a career on mistrust

Zero Trust: Verify everything. Assume breach. Never trust, always verify.

But to be honest, you trust the library that you imported without reading, the SDK that someone recommended, the developer that wrote that service, the CA that issued the certificate, the auditor that reviewed the contract three years ago, the cloud that stores the keys. Trust has not disappeared anywhere. It just became invisible

Zero Trust is not a lack of trust

It's honesty about where exactly you trust and on what basis. This is documented trust instead of blind, that is, conscious instead of accidental.

For example: in the passage they don’t tell the people “everything is safe,” but they say that you are not alone in this. Go forward knowing that someone will support you

The real role of a security guard is not the one who shouts “everything is dangerous and don’t trust anyone,” but the one next to whom the team can work without being paralyzed by fear. That is, the person whose presence makes movement possible.

Information security is not the absence of enemies, but the presence of someone who sees them and still says: go
