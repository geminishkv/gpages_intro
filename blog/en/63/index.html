🛠 Course for MIPT on secure development

Salute,

Начнем неделю с прикольного, я тут активно работаю над новой программой обучения (ну как, уже релижу) для Московского Физико-Технического Института МФТИ и думаю, что прикольно поделиться с тобой этим. Here you can read the pre-roll.

Let me remind you that I teach at MSTU named after N. E. Bauman @bmstu1830 at PS10 (although I myself graduated from PS8) and there is a course for the guys from Inseca.tech on Security Champions.

The course is aimed at developing technical skills and is aimed at providing a basis that will allow you to take a step towards mastering the concept of information security during development.

Program name

Information security at all stages of the software life cycle.

Basic information

The course is an additional professional development program from LANIT. The joint program between MIPT and LANIT is a response to the challenges of modern development, where security is no longer an option or a testing stage, but is becoming an integral part of the software creation culture. Training under the program is a unique offer for MIPT students and is aimed at training engineers (of course not related to technical knowledge).

Structure

Information security analytics

- Fundamentals of information security and information protection in information systems

- Vulnerability and threat analysis

- Formation of information security requirements

Safe development

- Shift-Left: integration of information security into design and coding processes

- Secure software development life cycle

- DevSecOps and Application Security in practice

Planned start: 3/11/2025

Duration: 6 weeks

Registration

We will look at vulnerabilities, information security analytics, architecture patterns, how to integrate into development and manage vulnerabilities, engage in triage, what tools are needed and why this will simplify our vision of the product.

As a result, you will be able to make a cool case related to practice and keep it in your piggy bank, and you will also be able to use it further: UML diagram of CI/CD pipeline, risk analysis, action plan to reduce vulnerabilities.

Join us, we will look at real cases and I will be glad to see you there 🙏
