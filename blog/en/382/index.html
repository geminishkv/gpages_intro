🏆 Managing Findings: the reality of metrics

When a toolchain starts pouring in hundreds of positives, the team quickly stops trusting them and the value of AppSec drops sharply. I think that it’s still worth figuring out how to treat this, so let’s take cases based on my practice.

At the conference, I explained behind the scenes how this is considered, why I chose this approach and what is important for it. Let's look at the metrics:

• False Positive Rate = (closed as FP / total positives) × 100% - on the ruleset thresholds of the tool (SQL Injection, XSS, SSRF, etc.)

Reko:

— ≤ 15% — the tool calculates it adequately (if you did it well and didn’t exclude everything)

— 15–30% — yellow zone, rules need to be adjusted

— > 30% — red zone, therefore an immediate audit of the instrument rules

— < 5% — check False Negative or something fell

• FPR by CWE: FPR(CWE XXX) = FP / all triggers × 100% - individual critical moments that are purely tailored to your specifics and stack. If FPR by CWE > 30%, then add sink/source whitelist, context-aware filters, annotation exceptions for the trigger rule.

Example:

- CWE-89 (SQL Injection) - low FPR with correct AST parsing, but high with regex

- CWE-79 (XSS) - FPR depends on HTML/JS/attr output context

- CWE-22 (Path Traversal) - high FPR due to false taint chains in ORM

— CWE-611 (XXE) — low FPR and frequent FN due to custom XML

- CWE-502 (Insecure Deserialization) - one of the most annoying FPRs

• Risk-based prioritization of triggers: Priority Score = CVSS × EPSS × Reachability × Business Impact

Odds:

— EPSS — probability of operation in 30 days (0.0–1.0)

— Reachability: 1.0 — endpoint is reachable from outside/from an adjacent circuit; 0.2 - isolated, no route

— Business Impact: 2.0 — external API, user data; 1.0 — internal service; 0.5 - dev/test environment

Example:

— Score = 7.5 × 0.65 × 1.0 × 2.0 = 9.75 → CRITICAL and emphasis on priority, which we exploit

• Where there is no CVSS/EPSS, we use: AppSec Priority = Severity × Confidence (confirmation of operation by level) × Reachability × Business Impact

Total: the minimum implementation process will be as follows

• Calculate FPR separately for each critical CWE - once per sprint, total once per quarter or H (half-year)

• CWE with FPR > 30% per rule audit, no shutdown

• For each trigger, calculate the AppSec Priority Score

• Threshold for the point where we hire immediately: Priority Score > 10

• Review Confidence coefficients for your instrument according to type 1.0 - High, 0.5 - Medium, 0.1 - Low
