Today we recorded a podcast with AKTIV.CONSULTING @aktivcons on news from the world of information security, where we reminisced and looked at classic models using AI.

It was interesting, there are a couple of meme moments, I hope you will be interested in what we discussed, we briefly discussed the following:

1. A third of Russian companies do not impose cybersecurity requirements on AI

More than 80% of Russian companies allow the use of generative artificial intelligence in software development. 95% of organizations recognize that generative AI poses significant security risks, and 40% emphasize their high criticality.

2. Attackers of Operation DragonReturn are passing off malicious files as an official tool of the Indian tax department. New utility secretly transfers IP addresses to third parties

3. AI in 2026: the collapse of the myth of autonomous hackers and hidden vulnerabilities of corporations. From January to April 2026 - more than 33 thousand attacks on businesses through fake AI services

A malware distribution campaign under the guise of popular AI services has been recorded, affecting small and medium-sized businesses around the world, including Russia. The classic vector of social engineering has moved to hype around AI tools.

4. Vibe coding breaks DevSecOps: the speed of code generation has outpaced the speed of its verification

Classic DevSecOps requires time for SAST/DAST/SCA and manual review before release. Vibe coding destroys these assumptions: the developer passes a large context to the model, which generates a block of code - and the cycle repeats faster than the AppSec team can respond. Plus, the data goes to the LLM, vulnerabilities are replicated across the project, secrets settle in the context.
