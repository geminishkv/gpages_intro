🏆 Summer School of Information Security at MSTU named after. N. E. Bauman

Salute,

Then a cool topic came up and I’m taking part in it, as you know - I’m a teacher (I didn’t think I’d be one).

MSTU im. Bauman launches summer educational sessions on information security for schoolchildren.

Not a club or a course on YouTube - a full-fledged full-time program on new university campuses with practice, CTF, real cases and communication with industry practitioners

Organizer: PS10 “Information Protection” with BAUMANTECH

Directions

• Grades 7–8: 1 week

• Grades 9–11: 1–2 weeks

Shifts

• 1st shift – from July 13

• 2nd shift – from July 20

• 3rd shift - from July 27

Internal

• Cyber incident investigation and digital footprint analysis

• Analysis of real cases from industry practitioners

• CTF and Olympiad tasks in information security

• Team projects with a defense at the final presentation, which can further get into the profile “Engineering: Information Security and Forensics” - a direct path to benefits upon admission (I recommend it for parents who want to promote their children)

• Excursions to information security companies and live meetings with experts

• Career guidance: what is the profession of an information security specialist from the inside

The first one got more than the plan - so I’m only sharing it now. There are still places for second and third

I will also participate as a guest expert - I will tell you about Vulnerability Management: how the process works, where it starts, what it looks like in real companies. We will consider architectural solutions as well. That is, we will discuss topics that are definitely not taught in school, but in the profession they are basic.

If your child is interested in information security, this is a good entry point. A week among people from the profession gives more understanding than a year of courses or retraining.

Power included. Takes place in the new campuses of Baumanka on Yauza.
