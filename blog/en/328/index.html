⚡️ AppSec breaks into the top: code is the new line of defense! ⚡️

Solar, the Fintech Association and the FinDevSecOps community summed up the results of the “Security UP: Security starts with code” award. And guess what? It seems that everyone has realized: now code security is not just a buzzword, but a real saving of time and money.

This is not just a matter of compliance, although regulators are closely monitoring. It’s just that vulnerabilities in the product hit the budget so much that it won’t seem enough. Therefore, the focus is shifting to preventive measures - companies build security into the code at the development stage.

On the case:

About 40 projects from retail and energy to insurance companies and government agencies. It is significant that the topic is important to everyone.

Who judged: Experts from the Bank of Russia, Yandex, Lanit, Moscow Exchange, MWS Cloud. Guys who understand the issue.

How much did they save: According to Solar, projects with a serious approach to AppSec saved more than 10 billion rubles, preventing many more problems. This is no joke!

Winners (best in their field):

* Russian Post: For an integrated approach to creating a scalable DevSecOps infrastructure.

* “Rosgosstrakh”: For creating a release security management platform

* “Jet Infosystems”: For initiatives in the field of Open Source solutions for containerization security and contribution to the development of the open-source framework. Open Source is karma +10.

* GKU MO "Information Security Center of the Moscow Region": for significant progress in the development of DevSecOps in the regions

* Faberlic: for implementing Security-by-Design principles in software development.

* SberTech: for the system of continuous monitoring of vulnerabilities

* T Plus: winner in the “New Heroes of Secure Development” category for the AppSec transformation project.

What’s also interesting: the winners will be able to test the AI ​​plugin as part of the Solar appScreener. Artificial intelligence in the world of AppSec is promising. We will watch the development.

The bottom line: DevSecOps is not just a trend. This is an effective way to reduce risks and save money. Learn, implement, stay informed, and may secure code be with you! 🛡️
